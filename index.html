My So Cute Baby

How did they grow so fast? Turn the baby photos already in your library into one growth video.

View on the App Store

For iPhone. In English, Japanese and Korean; other device languages show English.

My So Cute Baby finds your baby's face in the photos you already have, suggests photos from each stage, and lines up the eyes so every frame flows into the next.

Start with the photos you have

Import your photos and choose your baby's face. Preview the suggested photos and remove any you don't want. Next time, add only the photos you've taken since.

Every month, up to the first birthday

Make a video of everything so far or of just this month. Once your baby reaches six months, and later the first birthday, you can choose that milestone as the video period.

Make it yours

Choose the length, the playback speed and how smooth the transitions feel. Add a gentle 3D effect, pick one of six color looks, and keep or change the suggested music.

Clip videos

Collect your baby's moving moments into a clip video.

A canvas for every day

Cut your baby's photos into stickers and decorate a page for each day. Browse a monthly or weekly calendar, and a map for photos that have location data.

Made on your iPhone

Photo analysis and video creation happen on your device. Your photos and finished videos are not uploaded to our server.

Credits and ads

  • A growth video made from photos uses 1 credit at any playback speed. A clip video uses 2 credits.
  • Credits are used only once your video is finished, and they never expire. New users receive bonus credits once per device.
  • There are no full-screen ads when you make or share videos. Free accounts may occasionally see an ad when saving canvases to Photos. Accounts that have bought credits at least once have no full-screen ads.
